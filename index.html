Zenkai SMP for Android

Privacy Policy

Effective September 29, 2026

This policy explains how Enix Studios Ltda (“we”) handles information when you use the Zenkai SMP app for Android (package net.enixstudios.zenkaismp) and when you play on the Zenkai SMP server (play.zenkaismp.net) through it. Zenkai SMP is not an official Minecraft product and is not approved by or associated with Mojang or Microsoft.

1. Who we are

Enix Studios Ltda, based in Brazil, is responsible for the app and the server. For any privacy question or request, write to contato@enixstudios.com.

2. Information stored on your device

  • Sign-in session. When you sign in with Microsoft, the app stores the session tokens and account details it needs to keep you signed in and start the game: your Minecraft username, account identifier (UUID) and skin. You type your password only on Microsoft’s sign-in page; the app never sees it.
  • Game data. The game files, mods, settings, control layouts and worlds that the app downloads or creates.
  • App settings. Your language, the version of the terms you accepted and your performance settings.
  • Logs. Technical logs from the launcher and the game. They stay on your device unless you choose to share one, for example with our support team.

Signing out removes the saved session. Uninstalling the app removes the data it keeps in its own folders; files you exported or shared elsewhere are not affected.

3. Information handled by Microsoft and Mojang

Microsoft handles sign-in (Microsoft account, Xbox Live and Minecraft services). The app downloads the game files and your skin image from Mojang and Microsoft servers, and some game libraries from public software repositories. These providers receive your IP address and the technical details needed to answer each request. See the Microsoft Privacy Statement.

4. Information handled by the Zenkai SMP server

When you connect to play.zenkaismp.net, our servers process:

  • your Minecraft username, account identifier (UUID) and skin;
  • your IP address and connection details, such as the game version and the mods used to connect;
  • gameplay data, such as your character, stats, quest and saga progress, inventory and items, in-game currency and market activity, land claims and clan membership;
  • the chat messages and commands you send;
  • moderation records, such as reports, warnings and bans.

We use this information to run the game and keep your progress across our servers, to prevent cheating, fraud and abuse, to enforce the community rules, to provide support and to keep the service secure. Other players can see your username, skin, chat messages and in-game actions.

5. What the app does not do

The app shows no ads, does not use the advertising ID, includes no third-party analytics and does not upload usage statistics. It does not access your microphone, camera, contacts or location, and it does not sell anything.

6. Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only:

  • with the hosting and infrastructure providers that run our servers and databases on our behalf;
  • with Microsoft and Mojang, as needed to sign you in and download the game (section 3);
  • when the law requires it, or to protect players and the service.

7. How long we keep information

  • On your device: until you sign out, clear the app’s data or uninstall the app.
  • On our servers: we keep your player profile and progress while you play on Zenkai SMP. Server logs (which can include IP addresses, commands and chat) and moderation records are kept for as long as they are needed for security, moderation and enforcing bans. When you ask us to delete your data, we delete or anonymize it, except for records we need to keep to prevent abuse (for example, an active ban) or to comply with the law. Copies may remain in backups until those backups are replaced.

8. Security

Sign-in and downloads use encrypted connections (HTTPS), and the game connection uses Minecraft’s encrypted protocol. Access to our servers and databases is limited to the people who run the service. No system is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. International transfers

We are based in Brazil, and our servers and providers may be located in other countries. When information is transferred across borders, we apply the safeguards required by applicable law.

10. Children

Zenkai SMP is intended for people aged 13 and over. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13, or under the minimum age in their country. If you believe a child has used the service, contact us and we will delete their data.

11. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example, under Brazil’s LGPD, the GDPR in the European Union and the UK GDPR, or US state privacy laws), you may have the right to access, correct or delete your data, to receive a copy of it, to object to or restrict its processing, and to complain to a data protection authority. To use these rights, write to contato@enixstudios.com or use Request access or deletion in the app. We may ask you to confirm that you control the Minecraft account before acting on a request. We reply within the time required by law.

12. Deleting your data

Step-by-step instructions: How to delete your Zenkai SMP data.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the current version. If we make a significant change, we will let you know in the app.

14. Contact

Enix Studios Ltda: contato@enixstudios.com
Community and support: discord.gg/zenkaismp
